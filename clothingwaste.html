Clothing Waste

Clothing waste is when clothes are thrown away instead of being reused, repaired, or recycled. Many clothes end up in landfills, which harms the environment and wastes materials used to make them.

Causes of Clothing Waste

Clothing waste is caused by fast fashion, buying too many clothes, and throwing away clothes when they are slightly damaged or no longer trendy.

Effects of Clothing Waste

Clothing waste increases pollution and fills up landfills. It also wastes water, energy, and materials used to produce clothing.

Ways to Reduce Clothing Waste

People can reduce clothing waste by donating clothes, repairing them, reusing old clothes, and buying fewer but higher quality items.

Why Recycling Clothes is Important

Recycling clothes helps reduce pollution, saves natural resources, and reduces the amount of waste going into landfills.
